Discover CubeCar!

Meet CubeCar, the compact and portable car designed for university students. With it's lightweight design, automatic assembly, and innovative hover wheels, getting to class has never been easier!

Portable Design

CubeCar is designed to make transportation simple and convenient. It's innovative design allows it to transform into a lightweight cube, making it easy for university students to carry and store when not in use.

10-Second Automatic Assembly

CubeCar transforms from a compact cube into a fully functional vehicle in less than 10 seconds. With just one click of a button, CubeCar automatically assembles itself, eliminating the need for tools or complicated setups. Just click, transform, and you're ready to go!

Hover Wheels

CubeCar's advanced hover wheels allow it to glide smoothly over obstacles, uneven surfaces, and snow. Whether you're navigating a busy campus or dealing with harsh winter conditions, CubeCar makes every journey easier and more comfortable. No more worrying about rough terrain slowing you down!

All-Weather Protection

Don't let bad weather slow you down! CubeCar features a protective roof that keeps you sheltered from rain and snow while travelling across campus. Whether it's a rainy morning or a snowy winter day, CubeCar helps you arrive at class dry and comfortable.
